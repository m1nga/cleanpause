04 · MOTION — EVENING WIND

Slow drift, 30–60px across a whole scene. Crossfades of .55–.75s. Nothing bounces, nothing pops. The one fast thing in this world is the accidental gibberish—everything after it slows down on purpose. Reduced motion collapses every loop to a still, legible state.

05 · VOICE — SHORT, THEN SOFT

Don't react. I'm just cleaning you.

Keep something yours.

Caps declaratives for structure; first-person serif italic when the product speaks. No fake community, no invented numbers, no sport metaphors—the court is a place, not a reference.
